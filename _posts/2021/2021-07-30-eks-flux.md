This guide documents an [Amazon EKS](https://aws.amazon.com/eks/) environment
managed with [Flux](https://fluxcd.io/).

- Prepares the AWS account structure and creates an EKS cluster
- Installs base [Kubernetes](https://kubernetes.io/) applications through
  [GitOps](https://opengitops.dev/)
- Adds application workloads and includes cleanup steps

Read the complete guide at [Amazon EKS with Flux](https://ruzickap.github.io/k8s-eks-flux/).
