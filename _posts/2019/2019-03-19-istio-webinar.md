This webinar introduces [Istio](https://istio.io/) service-mesh capabilities on
[Amazon EKS](https://aws.amazon.com/eks/).

![Istio logo](https://ruzickap.github.io/k8s-istio-webinar/assets/img/istio.a24267ed.svg){:width="300"}

- Creates an EKS cluster and installs [Istio](https://istio.io/) with
  [Helm](https://helm.sh/)
- Deploys the [Bookinfo](https://istio.io/latest/docs/examples/bookinfo/)
  application
- Covers routing, fault injection, traffic splitting, observability, and security

Read the complete guide at [Istio Service Mesh Webinar](https://ruzickap.github.io/k8s-istio-webinar/).
