This guide demonstrates [Jenkins X](https://jenkins-x.io/) on a
[Kubernetes](https://kubernetes.io/) cluster in AWS.

- Installs Jenkins X using a [kops](https://kops.sigs.k8s.io/)-based Kubernetes
  environment
- Deploys the [Sock Shop](https://github.com/microservices-demo/microservices-demo)
  microservices application
- Covers [Jenkins X](https://jenkins-x.io/) operational tasks and cleanup

Read the complete guide at [Kubernetes with Jenkins X and Sock Shop](https://ruzickap.github.io/k8s-jenkins-x/).

View the source at [ruzickap/k8s-jenkins-x](https://github.com/ruzickap/k8s-jenkins-x).
